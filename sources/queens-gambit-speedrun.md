# Queen's Gambit Speedrun Source Index

Primary source: [Aman's Queen's Gambit Speedrun](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUjxDD7HNNTgUtmFtlWp2W3Lu9RKRziZv), 22 episodes covering the 400–500 through 2500–2600 rating bands.

The videos remain authoritative. The attached 159-game PGN was cross-referenced with English auto-generated captions. Timestamps below identify representative teaching moments; auto-captions can mishear chess notation, so positions were checked against the PGN.

- Slav's purpose: release the c8-bishop before `...e6`: [1100–1200 at 28:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gkat8uMqHqU&t=1728s)
- Standard Slav development with `Nc3`, `e3`, and `Nf3`: [1900–2000 at 18:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IJumW5CW5DM&t=1091s)
