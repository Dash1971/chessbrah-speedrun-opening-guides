# Orangutan Speedrun Source Index

Primary source: [Aman's Orangutan / `1.b4` Speedrun](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUjxDD7HNNTgZKIFKmMYmmsEYPBkuC_Wh), 22 episodes covering the 400–500 through 2500–2600 rating bands.

The videos remain authoritative. The supplied PGN contains 183 legal games; 153 link back to an episode, many with exact timestamps. The remaining games were matched by episode order, rating band, and move sequence. English auto-generated captions were checked against the PGN because notation can be misheard.

- Do not block c4 automatically with the b1-knight: [500–600 at 3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmyP0gIXP7A&t=225s)

- The `e3-c4` shell is what White wants in quiet positions: [1200–1300 at 0:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HewqyrsBO2c&t=13s)
- Use `b5` when it is attacked or gains a concrete tempo: [1300–1400 at 30:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhZFR728Vt0&t=1817s)
- When attacked, push; recurring `d4`, `c4`, `a4`, `Nc3`, and `Qb3`: [1400–1500 at 3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eVtZeMFaUhY&t=206s)
- `1...a5` is met by `b5`: [1400–1500 at 0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eVtZeMFaUhY&t=14s)
- The b5 pawn often needs `a4` to stabilise it: [1700–1800 at 0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ah6OXpQBRfE&t=14s)
- `Rb1` as the natural open-file activation: [2000–2100 at 19:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=43xXioMmtYk&t=1151s)
- Centre pawns versus Black's queenside pawns: [2100–2200 at 20:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IeCobIaUmQs&t=1221s)
- A better pawn structure can justify a simple bishop retreat: [2100–2200 at 22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IeCobIaUmQs&t=1342s)
