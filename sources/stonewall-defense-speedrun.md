# Stonewall Defense speedrun sources

Primary source: [Aman's Stonewall Speedrun playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUjxDD7HNNTgUMR77Bdd3NGtvFXApHZkS). The Black guide separates the games in which Aman obtained a Stonewall from the many `1.e4` games where he correctly switched to a French structure.

## Episodes

The Defense material runs through the same [19-video playlist](stonewall-attack-speedrun.md#episodes). A supporting source used by the supplied PGN is [this earlier combined speedrun video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VAHDgpJTJ4w).
