## Representative teaching moments

- [0:09, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0UmYoFigy4&t=9s) — the four-pawn Stonewall formation.
- [0:31, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0UmYoFigy4&t=31s) — do not force the Stonewall after Black gets `...e5`.
- [2:52, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0UmYoFigy4&t=172s) — the problem c1-bishop and its `Bd2-e1-h4` route.
- [7:46, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0UmYoFigy4&t=466s) — `fxe5` and the role of `Nbd2`.
- [19:29, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0UmYoFigy4&t=1169s) — keep the attack on the kingside.
- [0:50, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYjp2MrfxkA&t=50s) — play `Nbd2` to prevent `...Ne4`.
- [1:29, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYjp2MrfxkA&t=89s) — put the knight on e5 and recapture with the f-pawn.
- [4:18, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYjp2MrfxkA&t=258s) — `Qf3`, `g4-g5`, `e4`, and rook-lift options.
- [8:45, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6yYOQWfQwCk&t=525s) — the recurring `Ne5` plan.
- [17:20, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6yYOQWfQwCk&t=1040s) — why an uncontested black knight on e4 spoils the attack.
- [1:41, episode 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rt93_cWZUUA&t=101s) — solve the bishop before completing the shell.
- [2:17, episode 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rt93_cWZUUA&t=137s) — use `e4` when Black denies `Ne5`.
- [4:22, episode 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0x1ktjbDO4&t=262s) — `e4` as one of the main ideas.
- [24:16, episode 11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yUFSsm7s3-E&t=1456s) — `Qf3` stops `...Ne4` while supporting the attack.
- [10:37, finale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8p6zBd3U8Tw&t=637s) — strong defence: exchange the light-squared bishops and establish a knight on e5.
- [16:38, finale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8p6zBd3U8Tw&t=998s) — return to the `Be1-h4` plan instead of rushing.
