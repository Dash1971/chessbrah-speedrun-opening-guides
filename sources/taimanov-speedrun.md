# Taimanov Speedrun Source Index

Primary source: [Aman's Taimanov Speedrun](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUjxDD7HNNTjZAD99gBAKVm_ZipXTtNYn), 20 episodes covering the 400-500 through 2300-2400 rating bands.

The videos remain the authoritative source. The links below identify representative moments supporting the guide's recurring themes; timestamps are approximate because they were indexed from auto-generated captions.

## Representative teaching moments

### The setup and the first branch

- The aim at the early levels—remember "where the pieces go" and the move order: [500-600 at 10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6qTfdsNGrY&t=640s)
- Core identity—`...e6`, `...Nc6`, `...Qc7`, `...a6`: [400-500 at 1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yVIB7_Q7sLk&t=81s)
- Why Aman chooses `2...e6` before `2...Nc6`: [400-500 at 12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yVIB7_Q7sLk&t=755s)
- Main move order—`...Qc7`, `...a6`, `...b5`, `...Bb7`, `...Rc8`: [1300-1400 at 45:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U70AB4Rqgw8&t=2704s)
- Why the queen moves before `...a6`: [1500-1600 at 2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGIh4Ex-NCg&t=125s)
- Against `c3`, attack the e-pawn instead of forcing the normal setup: [1100-1200 at 6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AmDns9omB7g&t=396s)
- Why `Qa4+` does not win the c6-knight while Black's d-pawn remains on d7: [1400-1500 at 9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XM23yxg9g7k&t=567s)
- Why `c4` changes the normal queenside plan: [800-900 at 23:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XwemT5NgoRk&t=1411s)
- Open Sicilian versus Closed Sicilian: [1600-1700 at 7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jIguRC-Zt7Y&t=465s)
- Against `Bc4`, "hit him with the pawns first": [700-800 at 21:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_usnXzNrTU8&t=1267s)
- If `Nb5` attacks the queen, `...Qb8` and `...a6` are often available: [400-500 at 17:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yVIB7_Q7sLk&t=1064s)

### `e5`, castling direction, and the English Attack

- Before `...Nf6`, make sure the knight has a square after `e5`; `...d6` provides d7: [900-1000 at 2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iivW3TFDIi8&t=179s)
- Develop first on the wing opposite White's king: [900-1000 at 12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iivW3TFDIi8&t=742s)
- Use `...h5` before `g4` to slow White's pawn storm: [900-1000 at 1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iivW3TFDIi8&t=93s)
- After `g4`, the separate `...h6`/`...hxg5` idea: [1800-1900 at 10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FmlhwZT7Vcs&t=633s)
- Answer `e5` with the `...b4` counter-threat in the KNeres post-game analysis: [900-1000 at 9:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iivW3TFDIi8&t=555s), repeated at [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iivW3TFDIi8&t=754s)
- `...Ne5` as the "perfect pivot square" against Bd3: [1900-2000 at 26:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NYTGOoqx7Tk&t=1574s)
- Delay castling until White's setup is clear: [1200-1300 at 15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nng2KyLqPqA&t=946s)
- Wait for the b1-knight before committing to `...b5`: [1900-2000 at 6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NYTGOoqx7Tk&t=364s)
- Prefer `...b6` when the bishop is the only objective: [1900-2000 at 8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NYTGOoqx7Tk&t=519s)
- Why `...d6` responds to `f4-e5`: [900-1000 at 50:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iivW3TFDIi8&t=3007s)
