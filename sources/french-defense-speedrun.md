## Representative teaching moments

- [0:20, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=20s) — `...c5` is the main central break.
- [2:30, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=150s) — Aman's active setup against the Exchange.
- [4:02, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=242s) — judge bishop exchanges by the resulting colour complex.
- [20:43, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=1243s) — attack the Advance pawn chain with `...c5`, `...Qb6`, and `...Nc6`.
- [24:39, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=1479s) — if White has not played `c3`, consider taking on d4.
- [29:18, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=1758s) — the closed centre can let Black delay castling.
- [0:32, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEuKTqShpE&t=32s) — against `Qe2+`, get castled and use the e-file.
- [9:24, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEuKTqShpE&t=564s) — short castling is safer; long castling is the aggressive choice.
- [14:59, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEuKTqShpE&t=899s) — the Exchange setup: `...Bd6`, `...Ne7-c6`, `...f6`, and `...Qd7`.
- [36:07, episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEuKTqShpE&t=2167s) — bishop trades depend on the pawn structure.
- [0:07, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=7s) — Advance with `4.Nf3`.
- [36:53, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=2213s) — Winawer Advance structure.
- [18:17, episode 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mdLIVFuqMTI&t=1097s) — Tarrasch structure.
- [63:10, episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU87kQCjL1g&t=3790s) — higher-rated Tarrasch game.
- [58:18, episode 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rH0atZvwYpw&t=3498s) — French structure against the King's Indian Attack.
- [83:25, episode 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rH0atZvwYpw&t=5005s) — Winawer `4.Ne2` gambit.
- [9:33, finale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eifuUWPKsP0&t=573s) — finale Tarrasch game.
- [68:19, finale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eifuUWPKsP0&t=4099s) — `2.c4` sideline.
