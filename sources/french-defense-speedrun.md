# French Defense speedrun sources

Primary source: [Aman's complete French Defense Speedrun playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUjxDD7HNNTgWqHZpTn_6s7_47bRMOo2c).

The guide was checked against the English auto-generated captions for all eight episodes. Obvious caption errors in chess notation—such as `97` for `Ne7`—were corrected by comparing the words with the board and the supplied annotated PGN. The guide follows Aman's spoken explanations; PGN comments are supporting notes, not substitutes for the source.

## Advance French

- [0:20, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=20s) — `...c5` targets the centre.
- [20:43, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=1243s) — pressure d4 with `...c5`, `...Qb6`, and `...Nc6`.
- [24:39, episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3h3t6GxTH8&t=1479s) — if White has not played `c3`, consider taking on d4.
- [0:20, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=20s) — Aman's stronger rule: without `c3`, take d4 immediately if the tactics work.
- [8:10, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=490s) — post-game explanation of why removing d4 lets Black control the centre.
- [24:18, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=1458s) — after `c3`, stick to `...Nc6`, `...Qb6`, and `...Bd7`.
- [25:22, episode 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dissPDPzWas&t=1522s) — the four-pawn chain and why it repeatedly collapses under pressure.
- [35:55, episode 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dissPDPzWas&t=2155s) — Black's basic theoretical response is simply to pressure the centre.
- [40:37, episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU87kQCjL1g&t=2437s) — ignore a harmless `Ng5` and continue undermining the centre.
- [2:43, episode 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rH0atZvwYpw&t=163s) — when White plays `g4`, examine `...h5` first.

## Aman's Winawer system

- [36:52, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=2212s) — `...Ne7`, not the main-line `...c5`; then `...b6` and `...Ba6`.
- [37:46, episode 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FdZxT8lKj0&t=2266s) — `...Na6-b8-c6-a5-c4`: one step back, two steps forward.
- [17:11, episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU87kQCjL1g&t=1031s) — restatement of the `...Ne7` and `...Ba6` system.
- [17:44, episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU87kQCjL1g&t=1064s) — `...h5` controls g4 so the knight can stay on f5.
- [26:18, episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU87kQCjL1g&t=1578s) — do not commit `...c5` early; trade light-squared bishops and play on light squares.
- [0:24, finale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eifuUWPKsP0&t=24s) — the series finale opens by restating the same `...Ne7`, `...b6`, `...Ba6` plan.
- [1:10, finale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eifuUWPKsP0&t=70s) — c4 and f5 are the knights' destination squares.
