# King's Indian Attack Speedrun Source Index

Primary source: [Aman's King's Indian Speedrun](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUjxDD7HNNTinraGDsjTkIh_NMnaIOr4K), 12 episodes covering roughly the 800–2000 rating climb.

The playlist includes the King's Indian Attack with White and King's Indian Defence with Black. This guide covers only the White repertoire requested here. The supplied PGN contains 88 legal White games: 51 have exact video timestamps, and 37 are explicitly unshown or associated with the surrounding run. English auto-generated captions were checked against the PGN because chess notation can be misheard.

## Representative teaching moments

- The series uses the KIA with White and KID with Black: [Part 1 at 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=0s)
- `1.e4` disguises the intended fianchetto; against the Caro-Kann start with `d3` and `Nbd2`: [Part 1 at 0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=55s)
- The recurring shell—`e4`, `d3`, both knights, `g3`, `Bg2`, and castling: [Part 1 at 2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=163s)
- `h3`, `Kh2`, and the eventual `f4` break: [Part 1 at 3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=194s)
- The c1-bishop often waits; the setup matters more than artificial development: [Part 1 at 4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=256s)
- `Nbd2` helps keep queens on the board: [Part 1 at 16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=979s)
- `c3` is the standard answer to a knight on c6 because it removes d4: [Part 1 at 17:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=1035s)
- The `Ng5` motif defends h3 and attacks Black's light-squared bishop: [Part 1 at 18:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=1114s)
- When Black has already played `...h6`, do not pretend the same bishop trap exists: [Part 1 at 23:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=1420s)
- `Nh4` clears the road for `f4`: [Part 1 at 24:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VY8ASpIIHYw&t=1456s)
- Against a `...Bg4-Bh5` pin, use `Qe1`, `Nh4`, and then `f4/g4`: [Part 2 at 0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DHVs24fjic&t=55s)
- A closed centre makes the kingside pawn expansion more coherent: [Part 2 at 2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DHVs24fjic&t=150s)
- Develop `Nbd2` before allowing `...Bxf3` when you want a knight recapture: [Part 2 at 24:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DHVs24fjic&t=1471s)
- Play `c3` before `Qe1` so `...Nd4` does not send the queen backwards: [Part 2 at 34:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DHVs24fjic&t=2082s)
- If Black castles long, switch to queenside expansion: [Part 2 at 26:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DHVs24fjic&t=1579s)
- The Caro-Kann move order in a complete game: [Part 5 at 0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UFM19j-hSE&t=27s)
- The Sicilian and French often reach the same KIA shell: [Part 6 at 0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LjHNDCaNiNg&t=32s)
- Against the French, `d3` and `Nbd2` again avoid the early queen-trade route: [Part 7 at 0:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qrQ0Oe3Ylk&t=20s)
- The Scandinavian is the major exception that interrupts the pure KIA move order: [Part 9 at 18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4CQtns8Vpk&t=1134s)
- The mature `c3`, `Qe1`, `Nh4`, `Kh2`, and `Nf5` sequence: [Part 9 at 22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4CQtns8Vpk&t=1345s)
- Against a non-threatening setup, Aman sometimes plays `f4` before `Nf3`: [Part 9 at 42:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4CQtns8Vpk&t=2562s)
- French structures call for `e5` and a kingside reroute: [Part 10 at 6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LkSzlala8EE&t=410s)
- The speedrun's purpose is an easy-to-use setup that remains familiar as rating rises: [Part 10 at 31:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LkSzlala8EE&t=1869s)
- At 1800+, `c3` still precedes `Nh4`, `f4`, and `gxf4`: [Part 11 at 0:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E8PImgGik5I&t=42s)
- `Kh2` both protects h3 and steps off the a7-g1 diagonal before `f4`: [Part 12 at 11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZ7B7_6M3d0&t=716s)
- The late-series summary remains `c3`, `Nh4`, `Kh2`, `f4`, and `f5`: [Part 12 at 17:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZ7B7_6M3d0&t=1039s)
- Preserve the fianchetto bishop; giving it up casually leaves the king's colour complex weak: [Part 12 at 55:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZ7B7_6M3d0&t=3343s)
